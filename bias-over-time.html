Since 2016, viewers repeat old voting friendships far more than juries

Jury Televote

Favoritism is how far one country's vote is from what the other countries gave the same song. If a song averages 5 points and Denmark gives it 12, Denmark's favoritism is +7.

Favoritism has not grown; only the number of points has. Where old friendships do show up in the votes, it is mostly the televote.

Favoritism, in points: the average size of the gap between a country's vote and the other countries' average, plus or minus. More points per country make every gap bigger.

Adjusted: each year's gaps divided by how widely that year's points are spread (the standard deviation). This removes the scoring system, so all years can be compared.

Note: this measures any disagreement with the other countries, whether taste, chance or friendship. The chart below tests friendship on its own.

For each pair of countries, e.g. Greece → Cyprus, we take the average favoritism in 2004–2015, when jury and televote were combined. That is the "old friendship".

Each year since 2016, the jury's and the televote's favoritism is compared with it (correlation). Mean: televote 0.54, jury 0.22.
