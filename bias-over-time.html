Jury Televote

Raw favoritism rises each time the scoring system hands out more points. Standardised for the spread of points, it stays level across all periods.

What has changed is who repeats old preferences. Since 2016 the televote follows the established voting patterns about twice as closely as the jury.

Each bar is the mean absolute favoritism for one voting system. Raw is in points. Standardised divides each year by the standard deviation of the points awarded, which removes the effect of the scoring system.

How closely each year's jury and televote favoritism follows the country pairs' mean favoritism in 2004–2015, when the two were combined. 1 = same pattern, 0 = no relation.

Mean 2016–2023: televote 0.54, jury 0.22. Juries depart from the average as much as viewers do, but much less along established friendships.
