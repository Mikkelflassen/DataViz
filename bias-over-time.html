Favoritism: the points a country gave a song minus the average the other countries gave it. Greece gives Cyprus 12, the others give Cyprus 3 on average: +9.

Extra points: how many more points a country gave a song than the other countries gave the same song. Norway gives Sweden 10, the others give Sweden 4 on average: +6.

Each line: the average over all votes to a country in the same region, over five contests. Dashed: votes between regions. Hover a region's name for its countries.

The same measure since 2016, when the jury and the televote each give their own points.

Points standardised to the 12-point scale, as on the other pages: each year is scaled to the spread of an average 1975–2015 final.
