Raw favoritism rises each time the scoring system hands out more points. Standardised for the spread of points, it stays level across all periods.

Each bar is the mean absolute favoritism for one voting system. Raw is in points. Standardised divides each year by the standard deviation of the points awarded, which removes the effect of the scoring system.
