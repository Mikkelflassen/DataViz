Blocs

    Blocs are derived from the votes by hierarchical clustering. Countries below the threshold remain unassigned.

    Hover a bloc, country or cell to highlight it in all views.

    Finals only. Minimum shared finals per pair: 5 for ranges of 20+ years, 3 for 8–19 years, 1 otherwise. Countries with too little data in the range are excluded.

    Average-linkage clustering on mutual favoritism. A cluster is a bloc when the mean favoritism between its members meets the threshold; members below it are removed. Up to eight blocs are shown.

    Rows give, columns receive; colours as on the map page. Countries are ordered by the clustering, so blocs appear as squares on the diagonal.

    A matrix is used because almost every pair has a value; as a network the connections would overlap.

    Bloc colours are categorical only. Grey: no bloc. Hatched: insufficient data.
