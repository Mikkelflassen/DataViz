      Sweden, year by year

      Jury Televote

      Since 2016 every country hands out two sets of points, one from a professional jury and one from the viewers' televote. So for each song we can see how much each of them liked it.

      Pick a country to follow it in the year-by-year chart, or click a dot or a name.

      Finals 2016–2023. There was no contest in 2020, so it is not on the slider. The range always covers at least two contests, because a single year says more about one song than about the country.

      The televote includes the "rest of world" online vote from 2023. A country cannot vote for itself, so its own row is left out.

      Each dot is a country that reached the final. Across: the average points it got from each country's jury. Up: the average from each country's viewers.

      On the diagonal juries and viewers agree. The further a dot sits from it, the more they disagree. Orange dots (upper left) get more from viewers and blue dots (lower right) more from juries. The background tint shows the same split.

      Both axes have the same scale and the plot is square, so the diagonal is exactly 45° and distance from it means the same everywhere.

      A hollow dot reached only one final in the selected years, so its position rests on a single contest.

      Bold names are the outliers: the gap (jury − televote) is more than 1.25 standard deviations from the average gap for the selected years, plus the selected country.

      Grey names are not outliers. They are added where there is room: the dot is clear of the diagonal, has no close neighbour, and the name fits right beside it.

      A name is only drawn when it cannot be mistaken for another dot's. It sits beside its own dot, or on a thin line leading to it, and keeps its distance from all other dots. Where that is impossible the dot has no name; hover it instead.

      Gap = average jury points − average televote points, per voting country. The lists show the five biggest gaps in each direction.

      Average points per voting country in each final, from juries and from viewers. A missing point means the country was not in that final. 2020 had no contest, so the lines run straight from 2019 to 2021.

      The shaded band marks the years picked with the slider.
