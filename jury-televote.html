Juries and viewers don't like the same songs

Since 2016 every country gives two separate sets of points: one from a professional jury and one from the viewers' televote. Each dot is a country that reached the final. Its position shows the average points it got per voting country from juries (across) and from viewers (up). The further a dot sits from the line, the more juries and viewers disagree.
